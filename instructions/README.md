# Attendee instructions

## Build your 90-day AI plan

Define a clear **12-month AI aspiration** and turn one initiative into a focused **90-day plan**.

All participants start with [Welcome to your lab environment](Exercise00.md) to sign in with the provided **AlexW** workshop account.

Continue with the [workshop overview and setup](Exercise01-00.md) to install the **AI Impact Coach skill** and start a new Cowork task. Then open the [workshop steps](Exercise01.md) and use the coach throughout. Steps 1-7 are together on one continuous Markdown page. In Skillable, select **Next** from setup to open that page, then scroll through the workshop in order.

Allow about **35 minutes** for Steps 1-5. Steps 6-7 are optional extras of about **5 minutes each**.

After Step 5, review the core-completion check on the same page. You can stop there; the review and executive pitch remain optional.

| Page or section | Time | Finish with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Welcome to your lab environment](Exercise00.md) | Required setup | Signed in to the virtual machine and the AlexW workshop account. |
| [Workshop overview and setup](Exercise01-00.md) | Before you begin | AI Impact Coach installed and a new Cowork task. |
| Step 1: Name the problem | 5 minutes | A confirmed business problem statement. |
| Step 2: Define your 12-month AI aspiration | 5 minutes | A one-sentence aspiration and one primary success metric. |
| Step 3: Identify your AI initiatives | 5 minutes | One existing initiative and one new opportunity. |
| Step 4: Prioritize your focus | 5 minutes | One 90-day initiative, a risk and mitigation, and an out-of-scope line. |
| Step 5: Design your 90-day plan | 15 minutes | A saved five-section plan and 30/60/90 milestones. |
| Step 6: Review your draft | 5 minutes, optional | A stronger plan with accepted review edits. |
| Step 7: Create your 1-minute pitch | 5 minutes, optional | A concise pitch for an executive stakeholder. |

> [!note] Install **AI Impact Coach** before starting a new task, then use **one Cowork task for the entire workshop**. You can stop after Step 5 once you have downloaded your Word plan.

## Workshop outcome

Take your saved plan with you. It should capture:

- Your **business problem and 12-month AI aspiration**.
- Two initiatives (**current + new**).
- One **prioritized 90-day initiative**.
- A **five-section plan**, one primary success metric, and **30/60/90 milestones**.

If you completed the extras, also save your accepted review edits and **1-minute executive pitch**. Validate all metrics and baselines before sharing the plan.
